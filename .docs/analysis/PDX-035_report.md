## 1. Summary

The landing page said every pack was "run against real tickets in a real repository". True,
and read as far wider than what happened. It now says what the numbers are over, derived from
the records:

> **What these numbers are over.** 12 tasks, all in one repository — tiangolo/full-stack-fastapi-template
> at cd83fc1. 6 tmpl-be (102 cells), 6 tmpl-fe (127 cells). Every frontend task adds a React
> component to that template; every backend task adds an endpoint to it, or a constraint on
> one. There is no mobile or client-app task here, no design task, no refactor, no debugging,
> no test authoring, and no task in a second repository. *This page used to say less than that.*

Two independent sources found this on the same day: the project owner, and an outside review
of the live site that scored it 6.5/10 and named hidden benchmark scope the single thing most
likely to lose a skeptical reader. That review's other two points land here too:

- **The figure now groups what it cannot separate.** A ranked bar chart says *this one won* at
  a glance whatever the whiskers show. Arms are sorted into three tiers — clears the baseline,
  not distinguishable from it, not measured — each with a caption saying what the tier means.
  On this corpus the baseline sits in the same tier as three of the five packs, which is the
  finding.
- **The page ends in a decision.** Derived: ponytail is the only arm clearing the baseline on
  frontend work and costs more per cell to do it, and no pack separates from the baseline on
  backend work at all.
